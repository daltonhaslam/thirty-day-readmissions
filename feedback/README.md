# Reader feedback

Every page links to the shared Paid & Penalized feedback Google Form (`FEEDBACK_FORM_URL` and the Site/Page entry IDs in `web/src/js/site.js`), owned by the project Gmail and documented in the private planning repo (`docs/feedback-form.md`). To stop feedback, turn off "Accepting responses" in the form; the links then open Google's closed-form page until the link is removed or replaced.
